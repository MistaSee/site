Bodge explains the day job

Four things he keeps coming back to.

He calls them areas of work. I call them the places he can’t leave alone.

AI and learning design

Taking difficult or very new ideas and making them learnable — understandable, engaging, and useful once the session ends.

Responsible AI adoption

Helping people and organisations use AI with real capability and real judgement: what it’s good for, where it misleads, and how to keep human decisions human. Not tool training.

Creative technology and prototyping

Prototypes, experimental interfaces, physical computing, games and AI experiments. Ideas get built and tested, not theorised about.

Speaking and workshops

Podcasts, conference talks, panels, workshops, facilitation and professional learning on AI, learning and technology.
