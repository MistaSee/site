Friction Maxxing: Desirable Difficulty in the Age of AI

AI can make the work easier. But when it does the thinking for us, what happens to the learning?

Which struggle is worth keeping?

This talk explores productive friction in AI-assisted learning: the difference between making learning more accessible and removing the mental work that makes it happen.

From a “fortress” that keeps AI out to a “climbing wall” that puts deliberate challenges in the learner’s path, it asks how we can design better AI tutors and better ways of assessing what someone has actually learned.

Two practical experiments in this space are DraftPunk and BrainBuddy, chatbots designed to support learners without simply handing them the finished answer.
